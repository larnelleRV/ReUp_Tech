Frequently asked questions

Everything people usually ask before buying or selling with us.

What do you buy and sell?
PS4s, PS5s, iPhones, and Apple headphones (AirPods, AirPods Pro, AirPods Max) — both brand new and secondhand.
How do I sell my device to you?
Send us photos and details over WhatsApp or Instagram DM (condition, accessories, storage size). We'll give you a fair quote the same day, then you drop it off or courier it in and get paid on collection or once it's checked in.
Is secondhand stock actually checked?
Yes. Every used item is tested before it's sold — battery health, screen, ports, buttons — so there are no surprises after you've paid.
How does payment work?
In Cape Town, you can pay cash on delivery/collection or by EFT. For Durban and Johannesburg, payment is by EFT only, and products are shipped once your payment reflects after we receive proof of payment.
Do you deliver outside Cape Town?
Yes — we courier to Durban and Johannesburg, usually within 2 to 3 working days. Cape Town collection/delivery is free within 30km of Bloubergstrand.
Is there a warranty on preloved items?
We're happy to answer questions about a specific item's condition and any warranty before you buy — just ask us on WhatsApp or Instagram.
How do I know what's currently in stock?
Check the "New products" and "Shop by category" sections on this site, or follow us on Instagram, where stock is updated regularly with real photos and prices.
